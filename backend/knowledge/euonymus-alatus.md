## Summary
Winged burning bush is a shrub that can form thickets, crowding out and shading other plants. Each plant can produce more than 100 seeds, which birds and small animals carry long distances. It usually grows in disturbed areas but has also been found in natural areas with few other invasive plants.

## History
Winged burning bush is native to Asia. It spread through commercial sales as a landscaping shrub, and also when floral arrangements containing its fruit were thrown away. On New York's list it is a regulated species: it may still be sold, but not released into the wild.

## How to handle it
Some local infestations cover up to 3 acres. Its seeds can stay alive for up to two years, so removal needs follow-up for several years.
