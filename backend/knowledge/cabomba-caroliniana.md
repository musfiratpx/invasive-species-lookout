## Summary
Fanwort is a fast-growing underwater plant that forms dense mats at or near the water surface. The mats clog waterways, block light from the plants below, slow water flow, get in the way of boats, change fish habitat and push out native water plants. New populations can quickly grow from small plant fragments.

## History
Fanwort is native to parts of North and South America, including areas just south of New York. It may be spread by aquarium materials and boats. In New York, only plants on Long Island have been seen flowering.

## How to handle it
Once established, fanwort is extremely difficult to remove from the bottom of a waterbody, and fragments can start new colonies. In New York City parks like Kissena, cutting, killing or removing plants without permission from NYC Parks is against park rules, so report it instead.
