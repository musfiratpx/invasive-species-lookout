## Summary
The veined rapa whelk is a large sea snail. Where it has invaded, such as the Black Sea, it has caused major changes to bottom-dwelling sea life and become a marine pest. Scientists are concerned about its possible impact on native species along the U.S. coast, where it is established in Chesapeake Bay.

## History
The veined rapa whelk is native to the western Pacific, including the Sea of Japan and the Yellow and East China Seas. It reached the Black Sea in the 1940s and spread quickly; in the U.S. it may have arrived as larvae in ships' ballast water or with egg masses moved with farmed seafood.
