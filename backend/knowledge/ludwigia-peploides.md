## Summary
Floating primrose-willow is a fast-growing water plant that forms dense mats on water and shorelines, blocking light, lowering oxygen and eliminating native plants and the feeding habitat wildlife rely on. Any piece of the plant can grow into a new one, and pieces are carried by water, wildlife and boats.

## History
Floating primrose-willow is native to South America and the southeastern United States. In New York it has been found only in the Long Island region (the LIISMA PRISM). People spread it through boating, changes to waterways, and dumping aquarium material.

## How to handle it
No management method is known to control it well in the long term. Because it needs shallow water, restoring deeper water may help. In New York City parks like Kissena, cutting, killing or removing plants without permission from NYC Parks is against park rules, so report it instead.
