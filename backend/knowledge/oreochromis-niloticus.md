## Summary
Nile tilapia is a fish that competes with native fish and is known to eat amphibians and young fish of other species. Where it has been introduced, it has been linked to the decline of endangered native fish.

## History
Nile tilapia is native to tropical and subtropical Africa and the Middle East, including the Nile and Niger rivers. It was brought to the U.S. for fish farming and reached open waters by escaping or being released from fish farms.

## How to handle it
Never release aquarium or pond fish into lakes, ponds or streams. NYS DEC suggests donating unwanted fish to a school, nature center, aquarium or zoo, returning them to the store, trading them with another hobbyist, or asking a veterinarian or pet store about humane options.
