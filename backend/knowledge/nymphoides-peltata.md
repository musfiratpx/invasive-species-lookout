## Summary
Yellow floating-heart is a water plant that forms dense mats on the water surface and can completely cover parts of ponds. The mats shade out underwater plants and lower the oxygen in the water, causing stagnation. Any piece of the plant can grow into a new one, and each plant can produce more than 100 seeds.

## History
Yellow floating-heart is native to Asia and Europe. It is sometimes planted as an ornamental in ponds, and its floating, hairy seeds and plant pieces can stick to waterfowl and boats. A huge population grows in the Hudson River marsh at Rogers Island.
