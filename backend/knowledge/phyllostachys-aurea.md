## Summary
Golden bamboo is a running bamboo that spreads underground and can form dense, impenetrable thickets that push out native plants. New York's official ranking assesses it together with yellow-groove bamboo and lists both as "Not Assessable", because no one has conclusively shown them spreading into the wild on their own, without people planting or moving them.

## History
Bamboos in this group are among the most popular sold, and they spread mainly when people plant them or move soil containing their underground stems.

## How to handle it
Small patches can be controlled by repeated cutting or mowing, several times each growing season for several years, until the underground stems run out of energy.
