## Summary
Slender false brome is a grass that grows in dense clumps with long, drooping, bright green leaves. It quickly forms dense colonies of a single species that crowd out other plants, and wildlife and livestock don't eat it. It grows in forest understories, meadows, stream banks and forest edges, often along hiking trails, deer trails and roads.

## History
Slender false brome is native to Europe, Asia and North Africa. It is not clear how it was introduced; it spreads mainly by seed, carried on mowers, vehicles, hiking gear, boots and deer.

## Seasonal differences
Its drooping flower stalks grow up to two feet high, and it flowers and sets seed between June and September.

## How to handle it
Cleaning footwear, hiking gear and vehicles after visiting infested areas helps stop its spread. Small patches can be removed by hand in spring, before it flowers.
