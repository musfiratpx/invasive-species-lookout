## Summary
Yellow-groove bamboo is a running bamboo that spreads underground and forms dense thickets with little or nothing growing beneath them. Stands larger than a quarter acre are known from Nassau County, New York. New York's official ranking lists it as "Not Assessable", because no one has conclusively shown it spreading into the wild on its own, without people planting or moving it.

## History
Yellow-groove bamboo is native to China. Bamboos in this group are among the most popular sold, and they spread mainly when people plant them or move soil containing their underground stems. Floodwaters can sometimes carry pieces downstream.

## How to handle it
Small patches can be controlled by repeated cutting or mowing, several times each growing season for several years, until the underground stems run out of energy.
